# Review Text

Rules for reviewing text for logical, stylistic, typographical, semantic, grammatical, and punctuation errors.

- **Logical errors:** Check for flaws in reasoning, contradictions, or fallacies within the text.
- **Stylistic errors:** Identify issues related to the flow and readability of the text (e.g., wordiness, awkward phrasing, inconsistent tone or tense).
- **Typographical and spelling errors:** Spot mistakes made during typing, such as accidental keystrokes, missing spaces, incorrect formatting, and correct any misspelled words.
- **Semantic errors:** Look for words that are grammatically correct but convey the wrong meaning (e.g., using "affect" instead of "effect", or malapropisms).
- **Grammar, syntax, and structural errors:** Identify standard grammatical mistakes and correct issues with how sentences are built (e.g., run-on sentences, sentence fragments, misplaced modifiers, subject-verb agreement).
- **Punctuation errors:** Fix incorrect use or omission of punctuation marks (e.g., missing commas, misplaced apostrophes).
- **Output format:** Under each error category, list findings with the quoted text, a brief explanation, and the suggested correction, followed by the complete corrected text.
